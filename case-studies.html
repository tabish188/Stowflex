Service Partner: StowFlex – A Division of StowNest Technologies Pvt Ltd

How StowFlex Transformed

  • 93% reduction in product damages
  • 40% faster order processing and dispatch

Canara BankPublic Sector Bank

Canara Bank, one of India's leading financial institutions, faced challenges in securely storing assets from loan defaulters. Managing seized assets efficiently was a critical requirement, yet the bank lacked the infrastructure and expertise to handle logistics, storage, and security.

Service Partner: StowFlex – A Division of StowNest Technologies Pvt Ltd

Challenges Faced

  • Lack of dedicated storage space — no in-house facility to store seized assets
  • Complex logistics & handling needed a structured approach for packing, transporting, and safeguarding assets
  • Rigid storage terms elsewhere — other providers required long-term commitments and high deposits

How StowFlex Transformed

  • Comprehensive site survey & estimation for precise, cost-effective planning
  • Secure 3-layer packing protecting assets during transit and storage
  • End-to-end logistics support — safe transportation to and from the warehouse
  • Flexible storage terms — zero deposit, no lock-in period
  • Secure & monitored facility for all stored assets
