Default Customer Asset Storage: Challenges & Our Solutions

Problems Faced by Banks

Lack of Reliable Logistics

Finding a secure and responsive transport solution for asset pickup is difficult.

No Dedicated Storage Space

When customers default on loans, banks often need to seize property. While awaiting resolution or an asset auction, there's no secure place to store seized household items.

Handling Commercial Assets

Seized commercial assets like vehicles and equipment require secure, spacious storage that banks often lack.

Tracking of Seized Assets

Monitoring the status or location of seized assets is often manual and error-prone.

Final Decision Execution

Disposing or returning assets after resolution involves legal compliance and coordination.
